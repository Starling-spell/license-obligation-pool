Title: LicenseObligationPool — Source-Bound Reversible Checklists

## Notes / Description

LicenseObligationPool is a reusable reversible obligation multiset, not a graph or certificate gate. Active license entries contribute reference counts to four review features. Leader and validators independently fetch complete SPDX license text from a pinned publisher revision, verify byte commitments and interpret conditional redistribution obligations. Exact agreement binds the semantic vector to fetched evidence; callers cannot supply text, labels or confidence. Removal reverses only the stored contribution, preserving obligations shared by other entries. Uncertain or mismatched evidence marks coverage PARTIAL, never an all-clear. Account/pool namespaces, permanently spent slot IDs and immutable reports with chained history prevent substitution and replay. StudioNet proofs cover MIT/BSD aggregation, reversal, false-hash blocking, recovery and rejected cross-pool/replay attempts. This is a bounded review checklist, not legal clearance.
